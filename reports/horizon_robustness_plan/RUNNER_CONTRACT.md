## Immutable artifacts and process boundary

Write only under a declared campaign output directory (default reports/horizon_robustness_results/campaigns/). Validate resolved containment, avoid broad deletion, and never overwrite existing complete results. Use atomic/exclusive artifact creation; malformed partial attempts must be visible and safely recoverable, not silently mistaken for success. Record native child PID/start identity and input hashes. Resume must reuse completed immutable successes AND failures; retries require explicit distinct attempt IDs/reasons. If an old process is still live, do not launch a duplicate. Detect stale ownership safely without killing an unrelated/reused PID. psutil is installed in both environments.

The child solver process receives only serialized TrainingProblem plus backend/run options. It must never receive a future window or raw-data access instructions. Parent independently validates a returned layout against raw historical orders and recomputes its objective/rows. Serialize the complete layout and its hash before the scorer is given the exact next-n-order segment. Verify source sequence membership/adjacency in the parent; set that fact in the evaluator boundary record only after the check. Full-export snapshot creation and stream retention are the documented exception to a literal no-future-file-access claim, not training features.

Use subprocess.Popen with explicit interpreter and argument list, shell=False, no visible helper window on Windows. Track wall time and child memory; enforce a per-attempt building-plus-solving deadline and the campaign ceiling, and retain timeout/resource/error records. A limit with no solution is not infeasibility. Interrupted processes, corrupt outputs or missing allocations stay explicit. No silent fallback to a smaller catalogue, different solver/model, easier cap or alternate source.

Cache/reuse key must include exact mathematical input, backend/version, seed, threads, native time cap, solver implementation hash, solve mode and warm-start identity. `TrainingProblem.model_hash` describes the share-capped visits model; min_slack additionally depends on the target vector b and must include exact b in its solve identity. Keep full input/source hashes separate from mathematical equivalence, so identical NOM models across n or HIST+ACT with effective nu0 can reuse a solve without losing each row's provenance. Do not reuse a different mode or invalid version. Evaluation always uses that row's original b+delta, never diagnostic eta or the future incumbent's shares.

Each terminal case artifact must contain `case` (manifest row), `solve_result` (SolveResult dict or null), `validation` (raw-history independent certificate or null), `evaluation` (evaluate_layout dict or null), `reference_evaluation` (same future under the frozen incumbent, never a reset target), `status`, and explicit reuse/attempt provenance. Missing layouts yield null metrics, not zeros or dropped rows. Keep the min_slack objective distinct from visit counts and do not report a diagnostic layout as an accepted policy-feasible solution unless it separately passes original caps. Successful future feasibility and valid training/solver feasibility are separate fields.
