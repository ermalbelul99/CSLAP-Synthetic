## Options

1. **Authorize one more narrow fix round (fix round 3).**
   - Scope: F-036 and F-038, plus the four MINOR items.
   - The check author is general-purpose/sonnet. code-reviewer/opus then re-reviews only the runner, `check_governance.py:575-669` and the MINOR diffs.
   - If that re-review is not PASS, H8 applies again.
   - Cost: about 2 dispatches.
2. **Option 1, plus a standing rule (user decision U8).**
   - Gates that validate governance tooling (check scripts and their fixtures) get up to 2 further targeted fix rounds before H8.
   - H8 stays unchanged for every evidence, claim and manuscript gate.
   - This avoids stopping again when P1 to P3 check scripts go through the same loop.
3. **Accept the checkers as they are.**
   - Close P0 on a user waiver of the P0:G4 PASS requirement, with F-036 and F-038 disclosed and the git fixture runner disabled until fixed.
   - Not recommended: N3 would let a malformed DR pass during the weighted votes of P1 onward.
