# Q-011: Which dispatch roles may confirm a fix?

**Class:** E. The repository evidence decides it, so no panel is needed.
**Raised by:** ORCH on 14 Sep 2026, during a dry run of the P0 close on a scratch copy. The code re-review (seq 17, defect D2, `W/governance/results/P0-017-code-rereview-checks.md`) raised the same defect independently.
**Status:** RESOLVED (a)

## Question

Rule (c) in `check_governance.py` (`check_governance.py:516`) requires the dispatch behind every `fix_confirmed_by` to carry role `critic`. That leaves two readings of a code-review loop:
- a separate `critic` dispatch must confirm the fix, or
- the `code_review` dispatch that raised the findings may confirm it.

## Why it matters

F-001 to F-012 were raised by `code-reviewer`/opus in seq 6, with role `code_review`. The same reviewer checked the fixes in seq 17, again with role `code_review`. If that check is recorded as the fix confirmation, the current checker reports 12 rule (c) violations. P0:G4 cannot be MET, and the same pattern recurs for every check script in P1 to P9.

## Decisive test

- Plan §4.5 BCL step 5 (`WRITING_ORCHESTRATION_PLAN_20260914.md:301`): "The critic that raised a finding, or another critic, confirms the fix." Here "critic" names the reviewing function in the loop, not a dispatch-role label.
- Plan P0 step 5 (`WRITING_ORCHESTRATION_PLAN_20260914.md:460-463`) makes `code-reviewer` the reviewer of check scripts. For those scripts, the code-review dispatch is the BCL critic.
- `GOVERNANCE_FORMATS.md:21` lists `critic` and `code_review` as separate dispatch roles. No other loop exists in which a `code_review` dispatch could confirm a fix.
- ORCH's dry run on a scratch copy: after the seq 17 confirmations were recorded, `--phase P0` reported 12 rule (c) violations, one per finding, each of the form `finding F-0xx fix_confirmed_by seq=17 has dispatch-log role 'code_review', expected 'critic'`. It also reported 260 rule (i) path-length hits, caused by the scratch path being about 150 characters deep. The real root is 43 characters, and the fix-round real-root run shows no rule (i) hits.

## Options

- (a) Accept `critic` or `code_review` as the fix confirmer's dispatch role.
- (b) Require the fix confirmer's role to equal the role of the dispatch that raised the finding.
- (c) Keep `critic` only, and add a separate `critic` dispatch to confirm code-review fixes.

## Resolution

**(a).**
- It is the smallest change consistent with plan §4.5 step 5.
- (b) is stricter than the plan, which also allows "another critic".
- (c) adds a dispatch that repeats the code reviewer's work under another label, and the plan asks for no such dispatch.

`GOVERNANCE_FORMATS.md` §2 records the rule. The §3 clarification and A-003 still govern findings against ORCH-built artifacts.

**Conservative default if challenged:** (a), with the A-003 family rule unchanged.

**Implementation:** the P0 targeted fix round 2 by the check author, `general-purpose`/sonnet, with fixtures. `code-reviewer`/opus re-reviews it. ORCH does not edit registered check scripts.
