**Class:** J. A judgment within scope: how strictly `check_claims.py` decides which numbers in a claim must trace.
**Status:** RESOLVED

## Question

The P1 check-author brief sets the numeric-token rules for `check_claims.py`. Those rules exempt tokens that real claims contain, so a wrong number in such a claim can pass P1:G3. The exemptions are:
- A hyphen before a number exempts it. This hides the upper end of a range such as "3.23-3.35", and hides negative values.
- A slash before a number exempts it. This hides the denominator of "k/3", although the same brief says a `k/3` ratio counts as two integers.
- Integers from 0 to 10 are exempt. This hides pass counts (the `ho.pass.<ARM>.count` values 0 and 3), so a false "3/3" can pass.
- The phrase prefixes `n = `, `line ` and `lines ` apply without a word boundary. "mean = 3.231630" and "baseline 3.85" are therefore exempt. This part is recorded as finding F-053.

## Why it matters

P1:G3 exists so that every number in the claim register traces to an anchor or a document value. The pass counts, the ranges and the ratios are among the paper's headline numbers.

## Options, most conservative first

"Conservative" here means that more numbers must trace.

- **(a) Stricter token rules.**
  - A hyphen or slash exempts the following number only when a letter immediately precedes that hyphen or slash, as in `Q-008`, `C-3`, `n/a`.
  - A hyphen after a digit, a space or the start of the text is a range separator or a minus sign, so the number counts. A negative number compares with its sign, and both `-` and `−` count as minus.
  - In `digits/digits`, both integers count.
  - Integers from 0 to 10 are exempt only when the claim cites no anchor or document value whose unit is `count` or `bool`. Otherwise they must trace.
  - The phrase prefixes and the single-letter prefixes (`§`, `Q-`, `C-`, `F`, `H`, `D`, `U`, `P`, `S`) exempt a number only when a word boundary comes before the prefix.
- **(b) The rules exactly as the brief first stated them.**

## Conservative default

(a).
